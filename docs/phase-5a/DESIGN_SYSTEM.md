# Phase 5A design decisions

The attached BEND and Toolset design document governs this prototype. Use Instrument Sans, white surfaces, pale green-grey workspace and #236F72 for primary actions and focus. Preserve clear labels, thin borders and restrained radius. A soft seafoam fill indicates selection; avoid colored side borders and decorative dashboard cards.

The artwork is the primary surface. Compose keeps navigation at left and layers/properties at right. A specialist session keeps a compact project-aware bar above an untouched editor. A video layer and BEND actions are explicitly labeled as demonstrations until Phase 4 establishes actual capabilities. Status is text as well as color, and callbacks are owned by the eventual host.
